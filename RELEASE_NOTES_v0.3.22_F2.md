# Ainkrad 0.3.22.f2

## F2 scope

F2 grows from the accepted F1 checkpoint without deleting prior lived data or reducing Spark activity for performance.

- Three independent human foundations at new-world creation: Ainkrad, Rulid and Zakkaria, 10 founders each, seeded far-apart placement; Rulid has a real coast. Existing saves are not retroactively reseeded.
- Human pregnancy can produce 1–4 separate Sparks. Multiplicity is chosen once at conception. Quadruplet delivery has a 50% base maternal mortality roll. Fertility remains bounded.
- Divine gifts are potentials with lived mastery 0–100%; grant no longer maxes progression. Matching real activity develops the gift. Passive body gifts develop only from lived evidence such as birthdays, actual recovery, strain, avoided danger or a protection trigger.
- Gifts may combine and heritable gifts pass independently with bounded probability. Legacy strengthens one chosen existing heritable gift rather than chaining the menu.
- Exceptional nonheritable abilities: Immortality, Eternal Youth, Phoenix and Living Archive. Immortality forbids reproduction and limits voluntary knowledge transmission to knowledge acquired during the last 50 lived years.
- Divine punishments/curses are separate from gifts: Misfortune, Frailty, Empty Hands and Discord. Lineage curses may pass to descendants at reduced intensity. They alter conditions/outcome probabilities, not free will.
- Contextual prayers remain real records and now avoid mechanically locking repeated adult prayers onto one family topic when other live needs exist.
- Existing Work maritime implementation is preserved and extended rather than replaced: physical materials/worksite → vessel → voluntary boarding/cargo → water route → weather/damage/return/landing/discovery. Vessel design evolves through real prior building/sailing experience: coastal skiff → sailing boat → coastal ship.
- Performance work remains computational only. No population-based suppression of life/actions was reintroduced.
