# F2 Divine gifts, abilities and burdens

A grant is **potential**, not an instant maxed statistic. Normal gifts store mastery in `0..1` and grow from matching lived evidence. Gifts can coexist. Heritable gifts are evaluated independently for each child; `Наследие` raises the chance of one selected already-existing heritable gift rather than chaining the gift menu.

## Exceptional abilities

These are intentionally nonheritable and binary rather than ordinary mastery tracks:

- **Бессмертие** — cannot die; cannot reproduce; voluntary transmission is limited to knowledge acquired in the last 50 lived years.
- **Вечная молодость** — body stops biological ageing beyond mature adulthood; injury, disease and catastrophe can still kill.
- **Феникс** — one non-old-age fatal event is survived, then the ability is consumed.
- **Живой архив** — personal memory coherence is protected from age/stress erosion; knowledge still has to be acquired and shared causally.

## Divine burdens / curses

Burdens are separate from gifts and never write resident will:

- **Печать неудачи** — small penalties to real risky outcomes such as exploration/innovation/combat; it does not change the chosen action.
- **Хрупкость** — worse recovery and greater harm from illness/danger.
- **Пустые руки** — part of *personal* gathered resources/money earnings is lost; settlement warehouses and other residents' property are not magically deleted.
- **Тень раздора** — real social interactions are harder/more tense; affection and choices are not directly rewritten.

A lineage curse may pass to a descendant at reduced intensity. Exceptional abilities never inherit.
