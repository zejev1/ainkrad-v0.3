- System agents own normal operation of their domains without Cardinal.

- Cardinal may reside on removable/separate hardware while world persistence remains on the world host.
- Cardinal usefulness is a falsifiable research hypothesis; fair tests may find it useful, neutral or harmful.
- Autonomous agents may never be intentionally weakened to make Cardinal appear valuable.
