# Cardinal World Authority

Ainkrad v0.3.10 gives Cardinal meaningful authority over the **world substrate**, never over people.

## Separation of powers

`CardinalWorldArchitect` receives only aggregate architecture observations. It can prepare a proposal but cannot mutate the world. `IndependentWorldAuthorityGateway` owns the narrow mutation capability and re-reads the current world before execution. `WorldEngine` enforces the constitution again at the commit boundary.

The ordinary intervention gateway remains separate. A resource or habitat experiment is not silently upgraded into law-making power.

## Learned authority

Cardinal experience is reconstructed from append-only evaluations and outcomes. It cannot grant itself a level.

- world-rule design requires level 3 and at least 64 observation cycles;
- demographic stewardship requires level 4 and at least 192 cycles;
- catastrophe modeling requires level 5, at least 512 observations, substantial ecology evidence and successful evaluated predictions.

These thresholds grant permission to propose, not permission to execute.

## World laws

A law belongs to a non-person domain: geography, ecology, climate, resources, demography or cosmology. It also names one engine mechanism, such as frontier expansion, wildlife recovery, fertility support, resource regeneration, mystic resonance, weather volatility or catastrophe recovery.

New law IDs are allowed only inside this registered mechanism set. The newest authorized law for a mechanism becomes active, so a newly created law changes simulation behavior rather than becoming decorative text. Every law has a fixed numeric range; an amendment may narrow that range but cannot widen it.

Authorization requires:

- the earned `world_rule_design` capability;
- necessity of at least `0.72`;
- at least three independent world evidence IDs;
- a bounded value and expected outcome;
- a fresh world revision;
- the world-authority cooldown;
- no protected-personhood terms or target IDs.

The live architect currently proposes conservative amendments to frontier or fertility-support conditions after a full observation window. It cannot author a resident response.

## Catastrophes

Catastrophe authority exists because a long-lived world needs a tested path for exceptional systemic events. It is intentionally much harder to use than a law amendment.

The gateway requires:

- `catastrophe_modeling` capability;
- necessity of at least `0.96`;
- at least twelve evidence events;
- systemic scope with no resident target list;
- population of at least twelve;
- magnitude no greater than `0.35`;
- predicted casualty ratio no greater than `0.18`;
- at least four possible survivors;
- duration no greater than 840,960 canonical world minutes (96 semantic quanta);
- an explicit recovery plan.

The engine applies exposure by place danger and individual resilience rather than selecting victims. Actual deaths cannot exceed the authorized ceiling. The event retains a destructive phase and a longer recovery phase governed by the catastrophe-recovery law. Survivors remember the event; wildlife, resources, fear, awe and later recovery can produce new history.

The mechanism is implemented and tested, but v0.3.10 does not fabricate an automatic catastrophe merely to entertain the observer or accelerate Cardinal experience. A future automatic proposal must demonstrate the exceptional evidence above.

## Constitutional limit

Cardinal may change the conditions under which life unfolds. It may not change who a person is or choose what that person does next.
