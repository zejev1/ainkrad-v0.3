# Cardinal Research Memory and Hypotheses

Cardinal is the central research system of Ainkrad. Its memory must make it more capable of longitudinal reasoning without creating an invisible private reality that cannot be audited.

## Evidence first

Cardinal research state is reconstructed from the append-only Cardinal journal. The source evidence remains:

- prior evaluations;
- gateway-backed intervention records reconciled into the journal;
- measured outcomes;
- Auditor records.

The active reasoning window may be bounded for performance. Bounded read windows are not permission to delete the historical evidence behind them.

## No hidden mutable belief store

The current v0.3 research context is derived, not authoritative mutable state. Recreating Cardinal over the same journal must reconstruct the same compatible context for the same logical observation.

This is important for falsifiability: an investigator can inspect which records Cardinal used rather than trusting an opaque in-memory belief that disappeared or changed after restart.

## Evidence-derived experience

In v0.3.9 Cardinal gains experience from journaled observation cycles, ecosystem observations, evaluated outcomes and predictions whose expected direction was observed. Level and capabilities are recomputed from those records; they are not a writable score that Cardinal can award itself.

The initial capabilities are read-only world observation and autonomy protection. Trend reasoning, ecosystem observation and outcome learning unlock only after explicit evidence counts. Temporary habitat-support planning requires both level 2 and eight ecosystem observations.

Capability growth expands what Cardinal may analyze or propose. It never grants a writer for resident actions, goals, memories, relationships, personality or skills. A newly unlocked habitat proposal still passes through independent audit and gateway authorization.

## Compatible history only

A research context uses only prior evaluations produced under the compatible Cardinal policy and sensor definition. Changing the meaning of a metric or the reasoning policy creates an experiment/version boundary; old evidence may still be studied, but it is not silently treated as if it were generated by the new algorithm.

## Retry rule

Only evidence from a strictly earlier logical observation time enters the current research context.

If a Cardinal cycle at time `T` successfully journals its evaluation and the process then retries the same cycle, that already-written evaluation at `T` does not count as new supporting history. The retry therefore rebuilds the same context and reproduces the same logical evaluation ID/content.

## Hypotheses

A detected systemic problem records an explicit hypothesis containing:

- stable hypothesis ID;
- problem kind;
- severity;
- persistence across compatible observations;
- trend;
- confidence;
- supporting evaluation IDs;
- relevant prior outcome IDs;
- a concise claim;
- a falsifier.

A hypothesis ID continues across consecutive compatible observations of the same unresolved problem. When the supporting chain breaks, a later recurrence begins a new hypothesis rather than pretending uninterrupted persistence.

## Defer is a real decision

Cardinal has three decision states:

- `no_action` — no systemic condition currently qualifies;
- `defer` — a problem is visible, but current evidence does not justify intervention;
- `propose` — the evidence is sufficient to request a bounded intervention test.

For non-critical conditions, v0.3 normally requires three consecutive compatible observations before proposing. This protects world autonomy from one-tick noise and makes "observe first" operational rather than rhetorical.

Critical thresholds may bypass the normal persistence window. The threshold and bypass are policy-versioned and must remain visible to the Auditor.

## Falsifiable intervention predictions

Every proposal is bound to the hypothesis that justified it and includes a prediction:

- target metric;
- expected direction;
- minimum improvement;
- logical observation horizon;
- human-readable statement.

The independent gateway rejects proposals that lack a valid bounded hypothesis/prediction contract.

When the horizon is reached, the Auditor records the actual metric delta and whether the stated prediction was observed. That record remains `observational_only`; success or failure of a single before/after observation is not proof of causation.

## Learning from failure without pretending causality

Cardinal may use recent failed predictions as a reason to defer repeating the same intervention. The correct interpretation is cautious: recent post-intervention observations did not match the prediction. It must not rewrite that into "the intervention caused failure" without a matched control or another valid causal design.

This is the beginning of a self-correcting Cardinal: memory changes what it is willing to test, while all evidence and assumptions remain inspectable.


## Experimental discipline and autonomy budget

Cardinal must not confuse repeated action with learning. An executed intervention creates a test window whose end is the greater of the gateway-authorized effect duration and the proposal's prediction horizon. Until that window has washed out and the required outcome exists, a new intervention of the same kind is deferred as `experiment_in_progress`.

This is stricter than the gateway cooldown. Cooldown protects the execution boundary from rapid repeated mutation; experimental discipline protects the meaning of the evidence.

Cardinal also derives a recent intervention-density assessment from executed intervention records. In v0.3.6 the non-critical autonomy budget is exhausted after three executed interventions inside 16 logical ticks. The purpose is not to make the world artificially stable. It is to preserve periods in which the world can demonstrate endogenous recovery, adaptation or failure without constant Cardinal assistance.

A critical threshold may explicitly override the density budget. It does not erase an unresolved same-kind test or make overlapping evidence scientifically clean.

The active research window remains bounded, but unresolved executed interventions are mandatory context and cannot fall out merely because later rows filled the normal tail. The independent Auditor reconstructs the same dependency facts through a separate audit-context builder and stores its own versioned fingerprint in the decision audit.
