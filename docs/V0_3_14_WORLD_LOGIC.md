# Ainkrad v0.3.14 — world-logic recovery

This release restores a stable civilization substrate without scripting resident minds.

## Underworld-inspired boundary

- System laws define physical consistency: coherent settlements, valid habitats and civilization continuity priority.
- Residents keep agency over work, relationships, family, learning, belief, travel and exploration.
- Generation-zero founders may travel anywhere but keep Ainkrad as their permanent home. Later generations may voluntarily resettle and found new settlements.
- A village becomes a city from resident population, not from arbitrary map stage.
- Cardinal treats demographic collapse as a first-class emergency and does not accelerate the frontier while humans are below the continuity floor.
- Cardinal still cannot write identity, memory, values, relationships or decisions. Independent gateways retain mutation authority.

## Geography and ecology

Ainkrad is a compact settlement cluster: homes, market/commons and workshop are inside; fields/farms sit at the edge; wilderness regions are outside. Wildlife/monsters only recover in compatible biomes. Orc/ogre settlements require physically compatible hostile land origins rather than the latest arbitrary region.

## Recovery cohort and reset

A pre-v0.3.14 live world with <=7 humans receives exactly one idempotent ten-person recovery cohort through the independent world-entry gateway. Fresh live worlds start with ten balanced founders.

The operator **New world** command starts a new epoch with ten fresh founders and a clean map/ecology while the append-only Cardinal journal remains intact. Cardinal keeps all-time experience counters, but decision persistence/outcome context is filtered to the current epoch so old-world evidence cannot masquerade as current-world evidence.
