# Settlement layout references from the owner

Received 2026-09-11 while testing FIX4. The four supplied screenshots are visual references; their image assets are not embedded into the application or redistributed in this package.

The common direction is dense, irregular blocks around civic squares; winding connected streets and branches; variation in plot orientation and building placement; agricultural land beyond the built-up edge; and a street network responding to riverbanks and terrain. Enclosures and gates in some references are examples, not an instruction to grant every new settlement a completed wall.

Use population-appropriate scale: small settlements should not receive the huge market squares or monumental structures drawn in large-city examples. Preserve the earlier requirements of 3–6 metre ordinary streets, a central street up to 10 metres, physical walking paths matching visible roads, distinct distant homelands, stable IDs and ownership, and geometry recalculation only when construction changes.

The references do not authorize replacing resident decisions with scripted city growth. Settlement construction still follows existing material, population and autonomous-life mechanisms.

FIX4 changes clock scheduling and continuity only. This note records the owner's visual direction for subsequent settlement work; it does not claim that these pictured city layouts have been implemented.
