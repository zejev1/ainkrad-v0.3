# Persistent Personhood Contract

Ainkrad v0.3.10 uses a **Fluctlight-inspired** bottom-up personhood model. This is an engineering analogy, not a claim that the simulation is conscious or that it reproduces the fictional technology from *Sword Art Online: Alicization*.

## A resident is a continuing person, not an action selector

Every resident has a stable `identityId` tied to the world and resident ID. The hot world projection persists:

- origin: born in the world or entered through the independent gateway;
- biological age, health, life stage, expected lifespan and living/dead state;
- parents, children and generation;
- personality and learned skills;
- emotions, values and beliefs;
- needs, current goal, latest decision evidence and a multi-tick plan;
- home, current location and social relationships.

Append-only memories retain interactions, reflection, births, deaths, world events and witnessed omens. A bounded decision read is not permission to delete the older life history.

`identityId` and continuity do not change when a resident changes goals, ages, grieves, learns or dies. Death ends biological action; it does not delete the resident row, relationships, lineage, identity or historical memories.

## Lived change

Emotion, values, beliefs and skills may change through the resident's own actions and experienced events. Examples include helping, forming a bond, reflection, prayer, witnessing an omen, surviving catastrophe, birth and bereavement.

Cardinal is not a lived event and has no mind writer. It cannot assign a goal, choose a partner, create love, erase grief, replace a memory or set a belief. Environmental changes can create conditions residents respond to, but the response still belongs to each resident.

## Coherent intentions

Exploration and hunting can span several connected places. A resident may retain a journey plan across ticks instead of forgetting the destination after every scoring cycle. Severe exhaustion or resource pressure can interrupt that plan. The plan is world-owned and inspectable; it is not a Cardinal command.

## Generations

Birth is not a scheduled spawn. A new child can appear only when two living adults have developed a sufficiently strong, low-conflict relationship and both have adequate health, resources and freedom from acute stress. The world also enforces a cooldown and carrying capacity.

The child receives:

- a new stable identity;
- two reciprocal parent links;
- a new generation number;
- blended but non-identical personality, values, beliefs and initial skills;
- its own future decisions and memories.

Cardinal may amend bounded environmental fertility support only after earning the relevant capability. It cannot select parents or directly create a birth.

## Mortality

Residents age at a deterministic world-time rate. Health reflects recovery, deprivation, danger and frailty. Death can follow old age, illness, deprivation or a gateway-authorized systemic catastrophe. Close relatives and strong social bonds retain the deceased relationship and can form grief memories.

The world is therefore capable of demographic failure, recovery and cultural succession. Cardinal has something real to learn from, but it is permanently forbidden to solve demographic problems by replacing people.

## Permanent protected domains

The constitution fixes these protected domains in persisted world state:

1. identity;
2. memory;
3. agency;
4. values;
5. relationships.

The independent world-authority gateway rejects rule IDs that address mind, identity, choice, belief, emotion, memory, personality or relationships. Cardinal's world-architecture observation contains aggregate population and frontier state, not resident objects.
