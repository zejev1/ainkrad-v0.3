# Ainkrad 0.3.21-hotfix.8

This patch is based on published FIX7 (`8cb97f0`).

- Visible thoughts no longer expose option counts or percentages. Each choice
  keeps a first-person reflection from the resident's bodily state, emotions,
  leading value, risk, alternative and intention. Routine deliberation takes
  12–45 world seconds; consequential choices take at most eight world minutes.
  The coarse life-update quantum advances consequences and is not presented as
  days spent thinking.

- Exploration keeps the resident's own reachable target, prioritizes known
  wilderness over town services, and records a newly discovered region in the
  discoverer's personal place knowledge.
- Directly connected frontier trails remain locally perceivable up to the
  maximum procedural survey radius; unconnected places stay unknown.
- Route geometry version 3 rounds terrain-search corners into the same sampled
  curve used by both map drawing and physical resident movement. Old version 2
  roads rebuild once while preserving traversal counts.
- Private divine responses use the exact message, contact kind, profession,
  leading personal value, belief/fear and the named gift. Legacy adds an
  inheritance chance without replacing the original gift.
